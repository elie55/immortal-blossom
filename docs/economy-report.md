# Economy simulation

Generated by `tools/economy_sim.py --hours 30 --runs 20`: 20 players, each playing 30 hours in two 45-minute sessions a day. The simulation replays the data's rules (stats by level and realm, gear rolls and refining, EXP, drop tables, first-clear and star rewards, the Sect's timers, duties, the Market and the Fate Pond) with a simple player, and decides battles with a win model calibrated on the real engine (below). Enemy power comes from `rules:growth`: Health and Attack times 1, times 1 + (0, 0.45, 0.9, 1.35, 2, 2.8, 3.8, 5) by realm, times each class's multiplier (hd battle 1.438, hd boss 1.704, hunt hundun 1.459, hunt qiongqi 0.96, hunt taotie 1.856, hunt taowu 1.5, hunt xiangliu 1.399, shrine 1.444, story battle 1.44, story boss 1.487, trial 2.5, valley 1.292), and some stages' own `power`.

The player: two 45-minute sessions a day, 12 hours apart (the Sect works in between); four minutes of menus a session; Journey stages and first clears by hand, tried once the margin reaches 1; farming on auto where the margin is at least 1.25 (about nine wins in ten); Medicine King Valley swept at three stars (up to 40 runs a session while the team is under its level cap or short of Spirit Stones). Gear goes to whichever team heroine it helps most, judged as if refined as far as the piece it replaces, and refining spends what is left above a reserve of stones. Breakthroughs need the pill, the stones and, from Core Formation on, a won tribulation.

## The win model

A team wins when its strength, offense (Attack with critical hits) times effective Health (Health with Defense) summed over the team, reaches theta times the stage's strength (the same product for each wave, summed over the waves). Theta belongs to a class of stages and comes from real battles: the four founders on auto, without gear, against each stage below at every level until the lowest level that wins at least two battles in three (`tools/calibrate.cjs --bisect`). By hand a player needs 0.85 of the strength. Battles are noisy: the margin is multiplied by a log-normal draw (sigma 0.2) before the test, so at margin 1 a team wins half the time.
