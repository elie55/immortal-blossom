# Research notes

What comparable games do, their numbers, and what Immortal Blossoms takes from each, as of Nov 1, 2026. Every fact comes from a page listed under Sources.

## SPAM, the model for combat

SPAM is a free-to-play PvP card battler by the French studio Ticroco, released on Steam on October 7, 2026.

| SPAM | Immortal Blossoms |
| --- | --- |
| Four cards a deck, all on the board from the start; no hand, draw or mana | Kept: four heroines against up to four enemy cards |
| Players alternate turns and cast every ability that is off cooldown, in any order | Kept, with Harmony chains rewarding feeding order |
| Cooldown shown in a ring: 1 means every turn, 3 every third turn | Kept: S1 1, S2 3, ultimates 5 |
| Element points add 10% to that element's damage and healing and never decay | Phase Qi: +8% a point, earned by casting into the phase you feed |
| Each card has six elemental resistances | Replaced by the Five Phases cycle |
| Tox Shop on even turns: three Tox for Spamtons, a reroll, skip or a ban; Luck raises rarity | Fortuitous Encounters for Spirit Qi; later rounds bring rarer finds, and Luck is dropped |
| From round 20, every card loses at least 10% of its max health each turn | Heavenly Tribulation from round 12 |
| Three talents a card, one active, unlocked by duplicates | Covered by Dao Blossom petals |

## Epic Seven, the model for hero building

- Elements: Fire beats Earth, Earth beats Ice and Ice beats Fire; Light and Dark beat each other. Advantage gives +10% damage and +15% hit chance. Disadvantage gives −10% damage, −15% hit chance and a 50% chance of Glancing Hits, which deal 25% less damage and apply no debuffs. Immortal Blossoms keeps the idea with a larger, fixed swing (+30% and −25%) that reads at a glance on a card.
- Covenant summons cost 5 Bookmarks or 100 Skystones each. The 121st summon without the featured 5★ is guaranteed to be that hero, and there is no 50/50. The 5★ rate is about 1.5%, up from 1.25–1.4% in 2019–2020 breakdowns.
- Mystic summons guarantee the featured Moonlight hero after 200 summons, and that count carries over between banners. A free daily Friendship Summon gives low-rarity heroes, the model for Sect Recruitment.
- A full Covenant guarantee costs about $302–$318 in Skystones: the spending the Fate Pond replaces with earned currency.

With no money involved, the Fate Pond can afford a generous curve: 27 summons per 5★ on average in the spec's simulation.

## Games that dropped gacha

- Another Eden Begins (September 17, 2026) remakes the free-to-play Another Eden as a single-player, buy-to-play game with gacha and microtransactions removed. Its 18 companions join through the story, encounter quests and kinship bonds. The base game costs $40, with five DLC character packs at $10 each or $45 together. It is the closest precedent for Immortal Blossoms' business model and for the quest-recruit option in the open questions.
- Octopath Traveler 0 has over 30 characters, each recruited through a side quest, parties of eight (four front, four back) and a town to rebuild, Wishvale, with a dojo where benched characters earn EXP, the same idea as the Sect's Closed-door Caves. A reviewer noted that recruits rarely join the main story afterward, a trap the Journey should avoid.

## Other ideas borrowed

| Game | Mechanic | Use in Immortal Blossoms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Honkai: Star Rail | Only weak elements reduce Toughness; at zero the enemy is Weakness Broken until its next turn, then it refills | Boss Qi shield and Shatter |
| Honkai: Star Rail | Skill Points shared by the team, capped at 5 and earned by basic attacks | Spirit Qi as one team resource |
| Reverse: 1999 | A few card actions per round; two cards of the same rank merge into a higher rank | An idea for later: merging duplicate techniques in Secret Realms |
| Etheria: Restart | Shells, mascots that float beside characters and sometimes attack or advance the next turn | Spirit beasts beside the formation |
| Seven Knights Re:BIRTH | Auto play handles farming but not bosses; a reviewer disliked the unseen player character | Auto and sweep for farming, bosses worth playing by hand, and open question 2 |
| Genshin Impact | Six constellations a character, opened by duplicates; the 3rd and 5th usually raise a skill or burst level, the others add character-specific effects | Dao Blossom: six petals opened by duplicates or Blossom Dew |
